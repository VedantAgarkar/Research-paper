Heatmap Interpretation
Evidence-based answers to 8 research landscape questions

1. Which areas are already mature?

Three dimensions score 4+ on both Coverage and Solution Maturity -- essentially solved problems:

  • Person detection (Coverage 5, Maturity 5): YOLO-family detectors achieve 30+ FPS at high accuracy. No research contribution needed.
  • Feature extraction / CNN backbones (Coverage 5, Maturity 4): MobileNetV2, EfficientNet, YOLOv8 are off-the-shelf.
  • Fall classification on standard benchmarks (Coverage 5, Maturity 4): P11 achieves 97-99% on Le2i + CAUCAFall. The standard benchmark problem is saturated.

2. Which areas remain technically difficult despite substantial research?

  • False-positive reduction (Coverage 3, Maturity 2): P06 passes peer review with only 2 non-fall classes. Real-world FPR is unknown for every paper.
  • Occlusion handling (Coverage 3, Maturity 1): Every paper addressing occlusion receives only a PARTIAL rating. P07 confirms skeleton tracking breaks under real occlusion.
  • Non-fall activity discrimination (Coverage 4, Maturity 2): Many papers test it, none against staircase-specific activities.
  • Real-time inference (Coverage 3, Maturity 2): 98.13% of real-time claims are unvalidated (P12). Only 11 of 588 papers measured it on hardware.

3. Which areas are underexplored?

  • Night/low-light with standard RGB CCTV (Coverage 2, Gap 2.4): Only P05 covers night -- with non-standard IR/depth hardware.
  • Fall verification / temporal threshold (Coverage 2, Gap 1.8): Most systems treat detection as instantaneous. Only P10, P11 implement temporal verification.
  • Local/edge processing (Coverage 2, Gap 1.8): Relevant but only 33% of papers address it.

4. Which areas are highly relevant to our system?

Dimensions scoring 5 on Our Relevance (critical to our staircase system):

  • Pose estimation, Temporal modelling, Fall classification, Fall verification
  • False-positive reduction, Non-fall discrimination, Night/low-light
  • Staircase environments, Elderly evaluation, Real-world CCTV
  • Real-time inference, Low-latency processing, Local/edge processing, Resource efficiency
  • Local alarm, Remote notification, Human acknowledgement, Alert timeout, Alert escalation, End-to-end response

5. Which areas have the strongest evidence of unresolved problems?

  • Staircase environments (Evidence 5): Confirmed absent by two independent systematic reviews (P07, P12).
  • Elderly subject evaluation (Evidence 5): P07 confirms 95% of literature; P12 confirms explicitly.
  • Real falls (Evidence 5): Confirmed absent by two systematic reviews.
  • Alert acknowledgement/escalation (Evidence 5): P07 + P12 both explicitly confirm zero implementations.
  • Real-time hardware validation (Evidence 5): P12 PRISMA -- 1.87% hardware validation rate.

6. Which apparent gaps are probably false gaps?

  • Person detection: Not a gap -- fully solved. YOLO is the right engineering choice.
  • Temporal modelling in general: Well-studied. LSTM/GRU for fall detection is not novel -- novelty is in the staircase context.
  • Cross-camera generalization: Our system uses fixed installed cameras; generalization across cameras is less critical.
  • Privacy: Our system addresses it through local processing -- not a research novelty by itself.

7. Which combinations of dimensions appear most promising?

The intersection of Staircase Environments + Realistic ADL Negatives + Real-time Inference + Alert Escalation produces an absolute research void -- zero papers have addressed even 3 of these 4 simultaneously.
  • Staircase + Elderly + CCTV: Triple void -- each at 0% individually, combination at absolute 0%.
  • Staircase + ADL Confounders + FP Reduction: Critical evaluation gap -- where existing models will fail.
  • Real-time Validation + Staircase + Alert Pipeline: System completeness gap confirmed by P12.
  • Night CCTV + Staircase + Fall Detection: Addresses the 8:42 PM scenario -- no prior work.

8. Which 3-5 research directions deserve deeper investigation?

  • [PRIMARY] Staircase fall detection with staircase-specific ADL evaluation: First-of-kind, evidenced by two systematic reviews. Gap 5.0, Evidence 5.
  • [SECONDARY] Alert acknowledgement and escalation pipeline: Confirmed absent from all 12 papers. Gap 5.0, Evidence 5.
  • [TERTIARY] Low-latency end-to-end pipeline on consumer GPU: P12 defines 10 FPS threshold; no staircase paper measured it. Gap 3.2, Evidence 4.
  • [SUPPORTING] Night/low-light with standard RGB CCTV: Addresses target 8:42 PM scenario. Gap 2.4, Evidence 3.

Skeptical Gap Validation Statement

Staircase fall detection is not a gap because one paper failed to test it. It is a gap because zero of 12 analyzed papers, zero of 87 survey-reviewed papers (P07), and zero of 11 hardware-validated papers (P12) have experimentally evaluated a complete vision-based fall detection pipeline in a staircase environment. Two independent PRISMA-compliant systematic reviews independently confirm this absence. The staircase environment introduces technically non-trivial challenges (descent posture mimicry, banister occlusion, steep pose-estimation angles) explicitly acknowledged as degrading factors in the literature (P07 Section 5). This represents a confirmed research void, not a reporting gap.
