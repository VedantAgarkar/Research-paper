---
description: Prompt 5: Limitations
---

For every paper, identify limitations and failure modes.

Search specifically for:

* Explicit limitations stated by the authors
* Future work
* False positives
* False negatives
* Occlusion failures
* Lighting failures
* Camera-angle failures
* Multiple-person failures
* Staircase-related failures
* Confusion with sitting
* Confusion with lying down
* Confusion with bending
* Confusion with kneeling
* Walking-related errors
* Slow falls
* Fast falls
* Partial falls
* Person leaving the camera frame
* Detection/tracking failures
* Dataset limitations
* Generalization limitations
* Computational limitations
* Real-time limitations
* Privacy concerns
* Deployment limitations
* Communication/alert limitations

For every limitation distinguish between:

A. Explicitly demonstrated failure
B. Explicitly acknowledged limitation
C. Limitation implied by experimental design

Do not invent limitations.

Rank each limitation by:

* Frequency across papers
* Severity
* Relevance to our proposed system

Provide evidence/page references.
