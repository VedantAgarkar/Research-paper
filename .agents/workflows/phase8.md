---
description: Prompt 8: Attack your own research gap
---

Act as a skeptical peer reviewer.

Review the proposed research gaps generated from the literature analysis.

For every proposed gap, attempt to DISPROVE it.

Search the analyzed papers for evidence that:

* The problem has already been solved
* Another paper already implements the proposed approach
* The problem is actually well studied
* The proposed contribution is only an engineering integration rather than research
* The gap is caused by missing reporting rather than missing research
* The proposed novelty is too incremental
* The proposed system cannot be fairly evaluated
* The proposed contribution is not technically meaningful

For every gap produce:

VALID
WEAK
UNSUPPORTED
ALREADY ADDRESSED

Explain exactly why.

Then retain only gaps that survive the critique.

Do not protect the proposed research direction.
