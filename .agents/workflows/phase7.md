---
description: Prompt 7: Find the actual research gaps
---

Using the complete cross-paper dataset, identify potential research gaps relevant to our target system.

Our target system is:

A real-time CCTV-based elderly fall-detection system for staircase/common-area environments. The system continuously detects and tracks people, analyzes temporal posture and movement changes, distinguishes falls from normal activities, activates a local alarm, sends an alert to nearby security/contacts, optionally provides visual evidence, and escalates the alert if nobody acknowledges it.

Do NOT generate generic gaps such as:

"Improve accuracy"
"Use a larger dataset"
"Use deep learning"
"Improve real-time performance"

unless the literature specifically demonstrates why these are unresolved.

Instead identify gaps based on evidence across papers.

For each candidate gap provide:

1. Gap statement
2. Papers demonstrating the existing state
3. Number of papers addressing the problem
4. Number of papers NOT addressing it
5. Evidence of limitation
6. Why the gap matters
7. Technical difficulty
8. Novelty potential
9. Feasibility for a student research project
10. Potential experimental design
11. Risks of the proposed research
12. What existing literature could invalidate this gap

Rank each candidate using:

Novelty: 1–5
Importance: 1–5
Feasibility: 1–5
Evidence strength: 1–5

Do not declare something a research gap merely because few papers mention it.

A valid gap requires a defensible argument supported by the literature.
