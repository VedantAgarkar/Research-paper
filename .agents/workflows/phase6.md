---
description: Prompt 6: Build the master comparison
---

You now have structured analyses of all papers in the research folder.

Do NOT independently reinterpret individual papers unless necessary to resolve an inconsistency.

Construct a comprehensive cross-paper research matrix.

Rows = papers.

Columns = research dimensions.

Include at minimum:

* Detection
* Tracking
* Pose estimation
* Feature representation
* Temporal modelling
* Classification
* Dataset
* Realism
* Multiple people
* Occlusion
* Lighting
* Camera variation
* Staircase evaluation
* False-positive mitigation
* Non-fall activity testing
* Real-time performance
* Hardware
* Alert mechanism
* Local alarm
* Remote notification
* Evidence transmission
* Acknowledgement
* Escalation
* Privacy
* Explicit limitations

Use:

YES
NO
PARTIAL
NOT REPORTED

Do not interpret "NOT REPORTED" as "NO".

Then calculate how many papers address each dimension and the percentage of the literature addressing it.

Identify dimensions that are:

1. Saturated
2. Moderately explored
3. Underexplored
4. Almost completely unexplored

Produce a ranked list.
