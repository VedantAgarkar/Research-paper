---
description: Prompt 9: Turn the surviving gap into a research plan
---

Using only the research gaps that survived the skeptical review, develop a research roadmap for the proposed CCTV-based fall-detection system.

Structure it as:

1. Research problem
2. Research gap
3. Research question
4. Hypothesis
5. Objectives
6. Proposed system architecture
7. Novel components
8. Experimental variables
9. Baseline systems
10. Dataset strategy
11. Training strategy
12. Testing strategy
13. Scenarios to test
14. Negative/non-fall scenarios
15. Evaluation metrics
16. Ablation studies
17. Real-time evaluation
18. False-positive evaluation
19. Staircase-specific evaluation
20. Hardware architecture
21. Alert architecture
22. Acknowledgement mechanism
23. Escalation mechanism
24. Privacy considerations
25. Expected contribution
26. Risks
27. Fallback research direction

Separate:

A. Research contribution
B. Engineering implementation
C. Experimental contribution

Do not claim novelty unless supported by the literature analysis.
